SHARK: turn Claude into a Shark Tank for your idea

What it is

Ask Claude about your idea and it usually tells you it's great. This free skill does the opposite. It puts your idea in front of five tough investor sharks whose whole job is to find the reasons to say no, so you hear them before a real investor, customer or boss does.

Each shark asks one hard question, from its own angle:

  • The Customer Shark: who actually pays for this?
  • The Numbers Shark: walk me through your numbers.
  • The Royalty Shark: only offers money for a cut of every sale.
  • The Copycat Shark: what stops a big company copying you?
  • The Founder Shark: why you?

Every shark either makes you an offer or tells you exactly why and says "I'm out". At the end you get a scorecard and a list of fixes, ranked.

1. Download the skill

  • Download shark-tank.zip (for the Claude app)
  • Open SKILL.md (the same skill as a text file)

It's free. I made it, and you can change anything in it.

2. Install it in the Claude app (web or desktop)

  1. Turn on code execution: Settings > Capabilities. Skills need it.
  2. Go to Customize > Skills.
  3. Click +, then Create skill, then Upload a skill, and pick shark-tank.zip.
  4. Make sure the skill is switched on in your skills list.

Or install it in Claude Code

Paste this into a terminal (Mac, Linux, or Git Bash on Windows):

mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills/shark-tank && curl -o ~/.claude/skills/shark-tank/SKILL.md https://louiscreatesai.github.io/free-ai/shark/SKILL.md

Then start a new Claude Code session.

3. Pitch it

Start a new chat and say something like:

Shark tank: I want to build an app that turns your fridge photos into a week of recipes. $5 a month.

If you leave things out, the sharks ask. Answer like you're on the show, then take the heat.

4. Fix it and pitch again

When the panel is done, Claude steps out of character and gives you:

  1. A table: every shark, in or out, and why.
  2. Fix these first: the reasons, worst first, each with one thing you can do this week.
  3. An invite to pitch again. The sharks remember the last round, so you can see the offers change.

No skills? Paste it instead

Open SKILL.md, copy everything, paste it into any AI chat (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) and add your pitch at the end. It works the same, you just paste it each time.

Honest note

The five sharks are made-up characters inspired by the show's format, not the real investors. It's practice, not financial advice. A real investor might still say yes to something the sharks hated, or no to something they loved. But you'll walk in knowing the hard questions.
