YOUTUBE: 11 free Claude skills that run your YouTube channel

What it is

A free, open source pack of 11 Claude skills for YouTube. It is made by Jake Schincariol and released under the MIT licence, so you can use and change it. No signup, no API key, nothing to connect.

  • Scripts: one idea becomes a script. You get five hooks off 21 formulas, scored, then the spoken script. Command: /yt-script
  • Titles and thumbnails: checked together as one pairing so they do not say the same thing twice. Command: /yt-package
  • Comments: sorts your comments into piles, writes replies in your voice and tells you which one to pin. Command: /yt-comment
  • Niche research: finds what is working in your niche and ranks it by how far each video beat its own channel. Command: /yt-viral
  • Retention: reads your retention export and shows the exact second people left. Command: /yt-retention
  • The rest: /yt-edit, /yt-plan, /yt-shorts, /yt-seo, /yt-chapters and /yt-audit (the whole channel, ending in one fix).

1. Get the repo

The whole pack lives here: github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill

2. Install it in Claude Code

The easy way: paste this into Claude.

https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill

Install this skill, then confirm /yt-script works.

Or in a terminal (Mac, Linux, or Git Bash on Windows). You need git installed.

git clone https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill.git
cp -r youtube-agent-skill/skills/yt-* ~/.claude/skills/

Restart Claude Code and the commands are ready.

No Claude Code? Paste it instead

Open one skill's SKILL.md on GitHub, copy the text, paste it at the top of a Claude chat and add your idea. Most of it works. You lose the small Python tools that score hooks and read retention.

3. Make it sound like you

Spend ten minutes on the voice file. Copy templates/voice.md from the repo to ~/.claude/youtube/voice.md and fill it in. Or send Claude three of your own videos and say "write my voice.md from these". Every skill reads that file, so the results sound like you.

4. Use it

Start a new Claude chat and say what you want, for example:

/yt-script A video about how I edit in half the time

/yt-viral My niche is personal finance for students

/yt-package Here is my title and thumbnail idea: ...

Honest note

These skills are made by Jake Schincariol, not by me. I found them, and they are free and MIT licensed. They write, you upload: nothing gets published for you. The hook score is a guide, not a promise that a video will do well. Check what a skill does before you give it access to your accounts.
