# Sources for the tips in haggle.html

Every tip in the page was read on the date below (2026-10-23) from the page itself. Tips I could not verify were left out. The "Something else" card has no tips on purpose.

1. Consumer Reports, "Cut Your Bills by More Than $800 a Month", published Oct 9, 2025. https://www.consumerreports.org/money/saving-money/cut-your-bills-by-more-than-800-dollars-a-month-a7795453407/
   Used for: ask the customer retention team, say you are considering canceling, "hang up and call again", great deals are often only for new customers. Read 2026-10-23.
2. CFPB, "What should I do if I can't pay a medical bill?", last reviewed Dec 7, 2023. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-should-i-do-if-i-cant-pay-a-medical-bill-en-2125/
   Used for: check the bill is correct and owed, ask for an itemized list, look for duplicate charges, financial assistance ("charity care"), ask about an interest-free repayment plan. Read 2026-10-23.
3. IRS, "Financial Assistance Policy and Emergency Medical Care Policy, Section 501(r)(4)", page updated Jul 3, 2026. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/financial-assistance-policy-and-emergency-medical-care-policy-section-501r4
   Used for: a charitable hospital's written financial assistance policy must be widely publicized, with free paper copies on request. Read 2026-10-23.
4. FTC Consumer Advice, "Getting In and Out of Free Trials, Auto-Renewals, and Negative Option Subscriptions", dated Sep 2024. https://consumer.ftc.gov/getting-out-free-trials-auto-renewals-negative-option-subscriptions
   Used for: follow the company's cancellation instructions, keep a copy of the request and notes, dispute continued charges with your card company (chargeback). Read 2026-10-23.
5. U.S. Department of Labor, "Salary Negotiation Participant Guide", Feb 2026. https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/VETS/files/SalaryNegotiation_PG_Interactive_Feb2026.pdf
   Used for: research pay ranges (BLS), do not negotiate right after the offer, aim high on the range, other parts of the offer, no ultimatums, do not leverage one offer against another, get all terms in writing. Read 2026-10-23 (text extracted from the PDF).
6. FTC Consumer Advice, "Buying a Used Car From a Dealer", dated Apr 2024. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-used-car-dealer
   Used for: independent mechanic inspection, a dealer that refuses may not be worth buying from, get all promises in writing. Read 2026-10-23.
7. NerdWallet, "45 Ways to Save Money On Bills", updated Sep 30, 2025. https://www.nerdwallet.com/finance/learn/how-to-lower-your-bills
   Used for: a landlord may lower rent in exchange for a longer lease or you handling your own repairs. Read 2026-10-23. This is the one non-government, non-Consumer Reports source, and a single line.
8. Consumer Reports, "How to Lower Your Car Insurance Rates", updated Aug 18, 2026. https://www.consumerreports.org/money/car-insurance/how-to-lower-your-car-insurance-rates-a9179717041/
   Used for: shop around, ask your current insurer to reprice, a higher deductible lowers the premium. Read 2026-10-23.
9. Triple-I (Insurance Information Institute), "Nine ways to lower your auto insurance costs", no date shown on page (copyright 2026). https://www.iii.org/article/nine-ways-to-lower-your-auto-insurance-costs
   Used for: get at least three quotes. Read 2026-10-23.

## Left out because I could not verify them
- Any rent-renewal tactic beyond source 7 (the CFPB landlord page is about back rent, not renewals).
- Hotel rate and late fee tips.
- Success-rate figures for negotiating (found only in secondhand reports).
- Certified mail for gym cancellations (the FTC page ties it to a letter to your card company, not the gym).
- Current status of the FTC "click to cancel" rule. A court vacated it in July 2025 and the FTC has since asked for comment. Nothing in the page depends on it.
