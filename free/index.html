Stop paying $20 a month for AI: 3 free ways (and a bonus)

Most people pay $20 a month for ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro. For everyday use you can get a lot of that for free. Here's exactly where to go, checked on the official pages on 26 September 2026.

1. ChatGPT Free: unlimited text chats

OpenAI's own help page says free users get "unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards". The free plan runs GPT-5.6 Luna.

  1. Go to chatgpt.com and sign up for free.
  2. Chat as much as you want.
  3. For hard questions, use Think. In the mobile app, tap + and pick Think. It's rolling out on the web too.

Limits: file uploads, images, voice and data analysis have their own limits, and ads can show on the free plan in some countries. ChatGPT Plus is $20 a month and gives you higher limits and more models.

Source: ChatGPT Free Tier FAQ

2. Claude Free: Sonnet 5

Anthropic says "Anyone can chat with Claude using Sonnet 5 on Claude.ai". It's the same Sonnet 5 that Pro users get.

  1. Go to claude.ai and sign up for free.
  2. Start chatting. The free plan gives you Sonnet 5 (and Haiku).

Limits: you get fewer messages. Pro is $20 a month and gives you at least 5x more usage per 5-hour session, plus Opus and Fable.

Sources: Claude Sonnet 5 and Claude pricing

3. Arena: race Claude against Gemini, free

Arena puts two AI models on the same question and lets you watch them answer at the same time.

  1. Go to arena.ai.
  2. Click the mode menu (it starts on Battle) and pick Side by Side.
  3. Pick two models, for example claude-sonnet-5-high and gemini-3.1-pro-preview.
  4. Type your question and both answer at once. Swap in GPT, Grok or any of the 100+ other models whenever you want. There were 132 in the list on 26 September 2026.

The catch: Arena collects prompts for research and can share conversations in public datasets. Never paste passwords, personal details or anything private.

Sources: Arena FAQ and Arena privacy policy

Bonus: Google's newest Flash model, free in AI Studio

Google says "Google AI Studio usage is free of charge in all available regions", and it has Gemini 3.8 Flash. On Arena's text leaderboard on 26 September 2026, gemini-3.8-flash-high was #10, above gpt-5.5-high at #21 (a preliminary score).

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Choose Gemini 3.8 Flash as the model and start chatting.

Sources: Gemini API pricing and Arena leaderboard

Good to know

  • Plans and limits change. Everything here was checked on 26 September 2026 on the official pages linked above.
  • Free plans are great for everyday use. If you hit the limits every day or need the biggest models, paying can still make sense.
