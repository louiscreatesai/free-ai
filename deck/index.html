DECK: Make your next presentation in Claude for free, with my exact brief

What it is

Claude Slides builds a presentation for you. Anthropic says Docs, Slides and Design came out of beta on 8 October 2026 and are available on every plan, including Free. The tool is good. What you tell it decides if the deck is good, so below is the brief I use.

  • Anthropic's announcement: claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion

1. Open Slides in Claude

These steps come from Anthropic's help page for artifacts.

  1. Open Claude and start a chat. You can also ask for a deck in a chat you already have.
  2. Either ask for a deck in plain words, or choose "Output" in the message box and pick a template. You can also pick a template in the "Artifacts" tab.
  3. Claude builds the deck from your notes, a report, or the work already in your chat.
  4. If nothing shows up, check Settings, then Capabilities. Anthropic says artifacts need "Cloud code execution and file creation" turned on.
  • Source: What are artifacts and how do I use them?

2. Paste the brief

Copy this, fill in the blanks, and send it with your notes. The two blanks that matter most are who the deck is for and the one thing they should remember.

Make a presentation.

Who it is for: [WHO WILL SIT AND WATCH, e.g. my team, my class, a client]
The one thing they should remember: [ONE SENTENCE]
How many slides: [NUMBER]

Rules:
- One idea per slide. If a slide has two ideas, split it or cut one.
- Put the point in the slide title. The title is a full sentence that says the point, not a label like "Overview".
- Short sentences. Plain words.
- No filler bullets. Do not add three bullets to a slide just to fill it. A slide can have one line, one number or one picture.
- Every slide must help them remember the one thing above. If it does not, leave it out.
- Before you build it, tell me the order of the slides in one line each, so I can fix it first.

3. Download it

Click "Export" on the deck. Anthropic's help page says:

  • Decks export to PowerPoint and PDF.
  • Decks can also go straight to Google Slides as editable files.
  • On the Claude app for iPhone and Android you can ask for a deck, see it in the Artifacts tab and edit it. Anthropic's announcement also says you can change a deck in the Claude mobile app.

I did not find a line saying you can export from the phone app, so do the export on a computer. Sharing settings are changed on the web or in Claude Desktop.

  • Sources: Anthropic help page and Anthropic announcement

Why most AI decks look the same

If you only say "make it professional", Claude has to guess who you are talking to and what matters. So it fills every slide with the same safe bullets. When you name the audience and the one thing to remember, it has something to cut against.

Honest note

  • Checked: the plan, the steps to start a deck, and the export options come from Anthropic's own pages, linked above. They were read on 18 October 2026. Anthropic can change them.
  • Not checked: the brief is my starting point. I could not run it inside Claude Slides before posting this, so I cannot promise what your deck will look like. Results vary. Change the rules until the deck sounds like you.
  • Free plans have limits on how much you can use Claude. Check your plan's limits.
  • You check every slide and every number before you present. Claude can get facts wrong, and it can make things up that look real.
  • Never present a deck you have not read all the way through.
