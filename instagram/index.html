INSTAGRAM: 5 free Claude skills that run your Instagram

What it is

A free, open source pack of 52 Claude skills for content. It is made by Ootto and released under the MIT licence, so you can use and change it. These five do the Instagram work:

  • Reels: writes the hook, every scene and the voiceover. Skill: reel-builder
  • Carousels: one idea becomes a swipeable carousel with a cover that stops the scroll and a last slide worth saving. Skill: carousel-builder
  • Captions: the caption, the hashtags and the first comment. Skill: caption-and-hashtags
  • Profile: rewrites your bio and name field so visitors follow. Skill: bio-profile-optimizer (and profile-conversion-audit to audit it first)
  • Comments: a friendly public reply with no link, then a DM with what they asked for. Skill: comment-responder

The other 47 skills in the pack cover things like hooks, trends, a content calendar and analytics. You get all 52 with one install.

1. Get the repo

The whole pack lives here: github.com/Ootto-AI/claude-content-skills

2. Install it in Claude Code

Paste this into a terminal (Mac, Linux, or Git Bash on Windows). You need git installed.

git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/Ootto-AI/claude-content-skills.git && bash claude-content-skills/install.sh

It copies every skill into ~/.claude/skills/. Restart Claude Code and the skills are ready.

No Claude Code? Paste it instead

Every skill is one plain text file called SKILL.md. Open the skill you want on GitHub (the links above), copy the text, paste it into a Claude chat and add your idea at the end. It works the same, you just paste it each time.

3. Use it

Start a new Claude chat and just say what you want, for example:

Make a carousel about 5 mistakes new creators make.

Write the caption for this reel: (paste your script)

Rewrite my bio. I want visitors to follow, and here is my current bio: ...

Each skill asks for what it needs if you leave something out.

Tips from the pack

  • The hook carries most of the reel. Put the payoff in the first second or two.
  • The public reply to a comment never holds a link. The link goes in the DM.
  • Give Claude your own voice and your past best posts, and the results sound like you, not like a template.

Honest note

These skills are made by Ootto, not by me. I found them, and they are free and MIT licensed. Ootto also has an autopilot product at ootto.ai that runs these for you. You do not need it for anything on this page. Check what a skill does before you give it access to your accounts.
