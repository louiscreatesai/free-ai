PAYWALL: the free version of apps you pay for

What it is

A free search page. Type an app you pay for and see its free or open source version, one plain line on what you give up by switching, and the link. 56 paid apps are on the list so far, from CapCut Pro and Canva Pro to Photoshop, ElevenLabs, Notion and 1Password.

Open PAYWALL

Steps

  1. Type the app you pay for. Small typos and short names work, like capcut or photshop.
  2. Read the yellow line. It says what you give up by switching.
  3. Tap Open to go to the free app's own page and check it yourself.
  4. Only cancel when it is worth it. Try the free one first.

How it was checked: on 24 October 2026 every free app's real page was opened and read, open source projects were checked for stars, licence and recent updates, and the give-up line comes from what those pages say. Apps that could not be checked were left out. No affiliate links.

Honest limits

  • It is a fixed list that was checked by hand, not a live search of the internet. If your app is not there, it says so.
  • Some free versions take more setup, like installing it yourself or running your own server. The give-up line says when.
  • Prices, plans and features change. Open the link and check before you cancel anything.
  • Open source means anyone can read and use the code. Free means free to use with closed code. Source available means you can run it yourself but the licence is not open source.
