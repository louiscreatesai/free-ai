RACE: ask five AIs the same question at once

What it is

One free page. You type your question once and it opens ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity with it, in the accounts you already have. No new subscription, no keys, no sign-up.

Open RACE Download the file

It is one HTML file. Save it and double-click it any time, it opens in your browser.

Steps

  1. Log in once to the AIs you use, in the same browser. Free accounts work.
  2. Type your question and untick any AI you do not want. Your ticks are remembered.
  3. On a computer: press Start the race. The first time, your browser blocks all but one. Click the blocked pop-up icon at the right of the address bar, choose Always allow, and press Start again. Now one click opens all of them, side by side.
  4. On a phone: tap each Open button. Each one opens that AI.
  5. Compare the answers and keep the best one.

What each AI does with your question

  • ChatGPT: sent for you, the answer starts on its own.
  • Perplexity: sent for you, the answer starts on its own.
  • Claude: your question is in the box. Press Send.
  • Grok: shows your question and asks Send this message? Press Send.
  • Gemini: does not take a question from a link. The page copies it for you. Paste with Cmd+V (Ctrl+V on Windows, or press and hold, then Paste on a phone).

Privacy: the page makes no network requests of its own. No keys, no accounts, no tracking. Your question only goes to the AI sites you open, in your own accounts.

Honest limits

  • Checked on 24 October 2026 while logged out. These sites can change how their links work at any time.
  • Side by side windows only work on a computer. On a phone you switch between tabs or apps.
  • Questions over 2,000 characters are not put in the link. Each site opens and you paste.
  • Tested in Chrome on a computer and in a phone-sized Chrome window. Not yet tested on a real phone, Safari or Firefox.
