RACE

Type your question once. Open it in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity, in the accounts you already use.

Open as:

Computer: browsers let a page open only one new tab per click. To open all of them with one click, allow pop-ups for this page once: press Start, then click the blocked pop-up icon at the right of the address bar and choose Always allow. Press Start again.

Phone: tap the buttons one by one. Each one opens that AI.

What happens on each site

    Every button also copies your question. If a box is ever empty, paste with Cmd+V (Ctrl+V on Windows, or press and hold, then Paste on a phone).

    Questions longer than 2,000 characters are not put in the link. The site opens and you paste.

    Checked on 24 October 2026. These sites can change how their links work at any time.

    Privacy

    This page makes no network requests of its own. No keys, no accounts, no tracking. Your question only goes to the AI sites you open, inside your own accounts there. Your ticks are saved in this browser only.
