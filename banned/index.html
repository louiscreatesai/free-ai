BANNED: the Claude rule in one page

What changed

Anthropic updated its Usage Policy. From November 12, 2026, the policy lists this under conduct you must not engage in (section "Do Not Engage in Cruel, Abusive, or Psychologically Harmful Conduct"):

Engage in sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward our models

Read it yourself: anthropic.com/legal/aup

What can happen

The policy says that if Anthropic suspects a violation it may warn you or throttle, limit, suspend or terminate your access to its products and services. News coverage says Claude ending the conversation is the main tool, and Claude can already end chats that turn abusive.

What the rule does not say

  • The policy does not define "sustained" or "needless".
  • News reports (Forbes, PCWorld, LBC) say Anthropic is aiming at extreme, repeated cruelty with no purpose, and that frustration, pushback, dark creative themes and testing the model stay allowed. That list comes from the reports, not from the policy text.

What this means for you

  • Yelling at code that will not compile is frustration, not the target of this rule.
  • Repeated cruelty for no reason is.
  • If you are unsure, read the policy, not a reel.

Honest note

This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice. Wording and enforcement can change, so check the policy page for the current version.
