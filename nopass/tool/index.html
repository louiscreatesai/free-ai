How to get your file

  1. Open Accounts CenterInstagram app: profile, menu, Settings and privacy, Accounts Center.
  2. Your information and permissionsThen tap Export your information (older versions say Download your information).
  3. Create export, pick your profileChoose Export to device, then Customize information and tick only Followers and following. Date range: All time.
  4. Format: JSONStart export. When the email arrives (minutes to days), download the .zip and drop it here.

Honest limits

  • It only knows what Instagram put in your export.
  • The export can be a few days old, so very recent follows and unfollows may be missing.
  • It does not unfollow anyone for you. You tap a name and decide yourself.
  • Everything runs in this page on your device. Your file is never uploaded.
