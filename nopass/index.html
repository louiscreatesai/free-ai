NOPASS: see who does not follow you back, no password needed

What it is

A free page that shows who does not follow you back on Instagram. It reads the file Instagram gives you when you ask for your own data. You never type your password anywhere. Apps that ask for your Instagram password to show you this are not worth the risk.

Open the follower checker

No file yet? Open it and tap DEMO to see how it works with made-up accounts.

Privacy: the checker runs on your own device. Your file is read by the page in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. The checker page makes no network requests. Tapping a name opens Instagram, which is a separate site.

Steps

  1. Ask Instagram for your file. In the Instagram app go to your profile, then the menu, then Settings and privacy, then Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions, then Export your information. Older versions call it Download your information.
  2. Pick what to export. Choose your profile, then Export to device, then Customize information and tick only Followers and following. Set the date range to All time and the format to JSON. Start the export.
  3. Wait for the email. It can take minutes or a few days. Download the .zip when it arrives and keep it as it is.
  4. Open the checker and choose the .zip. If you already unzipped it, you can pick followers_1.json and following.json instead.
  5. Read the result. You get the people who do not follow you back, your fans (they follow you and you do not follow them), your mutuals and your new followers per month. Tap a name to open that profile and decide for yourself.

Honest limits

  • Not yet tested on a real Instagram export. I built it from published descriptions of the file and tested it on made-up files. If your file does not open, message me on Instagram and tell me what you saw.
  • The menu names above come from other guides and Instagram changes them from time to time. If a name is different, look for Export or Download your information.
  • It only knows what Instagram put in the file. The file can be a few days old, so very recent follows and unfollows may be missing.
  • It cannot tell you who unfollowed you. For that you would need two exports from two different days. The recently unfollowed list shows accounts you unfollowed yourself.
  • It never unfollows anyone for you.
  • The DEMO numbers are a sample with made-up handles. In the demo it shows 312 people not following back out of 900 followed. That is not a real account.
  • Your file lists other people's usernames, so keep the .zip private and delete it when you are done.
